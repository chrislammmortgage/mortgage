# Non-QM Bank Statement Loan Guidelines (Portfolio Loan)

## Overview
Bank statement loans (referred to as "Portfolio Loans" with clients) allow self-employed borrowers to qualify using bank statements rather than tax returns. Income is calculated from deposits over a 12 or 24 month period.

---

## General Eligibility

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Borrower Type | Self-employed (2+ years preferred, 1 year minimum with some investors) |
| Income Documentation | 12 or 24 months personal or business bank statements |
| Occupancy | Owner-occupied, second home, or investment property |
| Non-Occupant Co-Borrower | Allowed by most investors -- co-borrower's income can be used |
| Minimum Credit Score | Varies: 620-700 depending on investor and LTV |
| Max LTV | Up to 90% (investor dependent); 80% LTV is standard sweet spot |
| Max Loan Amount | Typically up to $3M-$5M |
| Property Types | SFR, Condo, 2-4 units, townhome |
| Reserves | 3-12 months PITIA (varies by investor, LTV, and loan amount) |

---

## Income Calculation Methods

### Business Bank Statements (12 months)
- Total deposits over 12 months
- Apply expense factor (typically 50% default, can vary by business type)
- Some investors allow CPA letter to support a different expense ratio
- Divide net figure by 12 for monthly qualifying income

### Personal Bank Statements (12 or 24 months)
- Total deposits over statement period
- May require sourcing of large or irregular deposits
- Divide by number of months for qualifying income

## Key Investor Comparison

### ARC (Angel Oak / ARC Home)
- 12 or 24 month bank statements
- Min 660 FICO (lower scores with LTV restrictions)
- Non-occupant co-borrower allowed
- Up to 90% LTV on primary residence
- Business and personal statements accepted
- Expense factor: 50% default or CPA letter

### Onsloh (Oaktree / Non-QM)
- 12 or 24 month bank statements
- Min 640 FICO
- Non-occupant co-borrower allowed
- Up to 85% LTV
- Flexible on business types
- Reserves: 6 months minimum

### Other Common Non-QM Investors
- **Deephaven**: 12/24 mo statements, 620+ FICO, up to 90% LTV
- **Newrez/New American Funding**: Smart Series products
- **Acra Lending**: Flexible guidelines, 12/24 mo
- **A&D Mortgage**: 12/24 mo, competitive rates
- **Flagstar**: Non-QM products available
- **UWM (United Wholesale Mortgage)**: Bank statement products

## Non-Occupant Co-Borrower Rules (General)

- Co-borrower does NOT need to occupy the property
- Co-borrower's income CAN be used for qualification
- Co-borrower must be on the note (and typically the deed of trust)
- Primary occupant(s) must also be on the loan
- Some investors require the occupying borrower to have some qualifying income
- Co-borrower's liabilities are also included in DTI calculation
- Co-borrower's credit score is factored in (lowest mid-score of all borrowers typically used for pricing)

---

## Lease Option Considerations

- Lease option agreements can demonstrate occupancy history
- Payments made under lease option may count toward payment history
- Deposit/option money may be credited toward down payment (investor specific)
- Need to document: lease option agreement, proof of payments, deposit receipt
- 6+ months of payment history under lease option strengthens the file

---

## Common Documentation Checklist (Bank Statement / Portfolio Loan)

- [ ] Completed loan application (1003) for all borrowers
- [ ] 12 months business bank statements (all pages, most recent completed month back)
- [ ] Business license or proof of business existence (2+ years)
- [ ] Business narrative / letter of explanation about the business
- [ ] CPA letter for expense factor (if applicable)
- [ ] Asset statements showing reserves and cash to close
- [ ] Mortgage statements for all owned properties
- [ ] Property tax and insurance declarations for owned properties
- [ ] Lease option agreement (if applicable)
- [ ] Proof of lease option payments (if applicable)
- [ ] Photo ID for all borrowers
- [ ] Authorization to pull credit
